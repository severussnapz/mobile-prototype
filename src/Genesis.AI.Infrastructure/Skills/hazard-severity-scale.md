## Hazard Severity Scale

**Purpose:** Apply the DCB0129 5-point severity scale consistently.

### DCB0129 Severity Scale

| Level | Label | Description | Example |
|-------|-------|-------------|---------|
| 1 | Negligible | No injury. Inconvenience only. | Slight delay in receiving non-urgent results |
| 2 | Minor | Minor injury, no lasting effects. Full recovery expected. | Non-urgent results delayed, requiring one extra appointment |
| 3 | Significant | Significant (but not permanent) injury. Some intervention required. | Patient receives wrong non-critical medication, requires treatment |
| 4 | Major | Permanent injury, hospitalisation, or long-term harm. | Patient receives contraindicated medication, hospitalised |
| 5 | Catastrophic | Death or permanent severe disability. | Patient dies due to missed critical allergy alert |

### Assignment Rule

When assigning severity:
1. Consider the WORST PLAUSIBLE outcome (not the average outcome)
2. Consider a vulnerable patient (elderly, paediatric, immunocompromised)
3. Document the scenario that leads to the severity rating

### Severity Justification Template

```markdown
**Severity:** {1-5} — {Label}
**Scenario:** {Describe the worst plausible outcome scenario}
**Vulnerable patient consideration:** {How does this affect a high-risk patient?}
```
